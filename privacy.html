Privacy Policy for BharatScanner AI

Effective Date: 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BharatScanner AI handles information when you use our mobile application. No user account or login is required to use the app.

1. Permissions and Information We Use

  • Camera Access: Used strictly when you choose to scan documents, photos, or text using your device camera.
  • Storage / Files & Media Access: Used to read files/images you select and to save scanned PDFs or images on your device.
  • AI & Document Processing: Any document or text processed through scanning or AI features is used solely to provide the requested functionality to you.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

BharatScanner AI displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may automatically collect and use certain device information—such as your device's Advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, and ad interaction/diagnostics data—to serve, measure, and personalize ads.

You can learn more about how Google uses data in advertising by visiting Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

3. How We Use Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal files or scanned documents to third parties. Scanned documents remain under your control on your device unless you choose to share or export them.

4. Third-Party Services

In addition to Google AdMob, if the app uses third-party services (such as AI processing APIs or Google Play Services), those services may process limited request or technical data solely to operate the feature under their respective privacy policies.

5. Data Security & Retention

We take reasonable measures to protect any data processed by the app. You can delete your scanned files anytime directly from the app or your device storage, and you can reset or limit your Advertising ID in your Android device settings.

6. Children's Privacy

BharatScanner AI is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
